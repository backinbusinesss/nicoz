About

I’m Nicoz, a developer who cares about details.

I build web experiences that are fast, visually striking and crafted with attention to every pixel. From streaming platforms to SaaS tools, I ship products people actually enjoy using.

Most of my work is design and engineering at the same time: I set the visual system, then write the code that runs it — no handoff, no approximation in the middle.

01

Product & UI design

Design systems, typography, layout and interaction detail — delivered as a working spec, not a static file.

02

Frontend engineering

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Fast, accessible and maintainable, with no framework unless it earns its place.

03

3D & motion

Three.js scenes, WebGL shaders and scroll-driven GSAP timelines that support the story instead of slowing the page down.
